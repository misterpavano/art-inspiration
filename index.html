Paint & Canvas — Lead
Extreme Macro Photos of Insect Wings by Chris Perani Layer Thousands of Images

Chris Perani stacks thousands of individual photographs to render the microscopic architecture of insect wings at a scale the naked eye could never reach. The resulting images sit somewhere between scientific documentation and pure abstraction, revealing structural patterns that painters have been approximating by instinct for centuries.

Colossal

There is something specific happening in these images for painters: the iridescent layering, the way color shifts with angle, the geometry hiding inside organic form. Perani is doing with a camera what glazing does with pigment. Worth a long look before the next session.
Mural & Pattern White Lace Proliferates Across Urban Spaces in Patterned Murals by NeSpoon

Polish street artist NeSpoon continues her practice of embedding traditional lace patterns into public architecture, painting directly onto walls, fences, and pavements in a white that reads simultaneously as folk art and formal geometry. The murals reframe everyday surfaces as textiles, asking whether decoration and fine art are as separate as the gallery world insists.

Colossal Memorial & Preservation The Tender Work of Preserving Renee Good’s Memorial

The careful, sustained effort to preserve a community memorial for artist Renee Good, exploring what it means to maintain an ephemeral artwork long after the initial grief has passed. An honest look at the labor of remembrance and whose work it tends to fall on.

Hyperallergic
Exhibition The Sartorial Is Political in “The New York Sari”

An exhibition examining the sari as a site of cultural negotiation, diaspora identity, and political statement in New York. The show argues garment is image, and that textile has always been one of the oldest and most personal forms of visual art.

Hyperallergic Museums & Access Meet the Woman Who Made Museums More Accessible

A profile of the advocate who fundamentally changed how major institutions think about physical access, audio description, and sensory programming. The work is practical and unglamorous and has touched millions of museum visits without most visitors ever knowing it.

Hyperallergic Art World New York Academy of Art Says It Will Donate Epstein Money

The New York Academy of Art announces it will donate funds received from Jeffrey Epstein, joining a growing list of institutions reckoning with tainted philanthropy. The announcement raises the harder question of where the money goes next and whether redirection is enough.

The Creative Mind — Lead
It Gets Easier: Creatives Share the Lessons That Changed Everything

A collection of candid reflections from working creatives on the specific moments, realizations, or hard-won shifts that changed how they practiced their craft. Not advice so much as testimony, gathered from people who made it through the part of the process that never shows up in the portfolio.

Creative Boom

The most useful pieces in this genre tend not to be the triumphant ones. The useful testimony is from people who describe the exact moment something stopped being a struggle and became a skill — and who can still remember what the struggle felt like. This reads like that kind of piece.
Design & Craft Béla Bezold Designs Eyewear Inspired by Lava and Symbiosis

Designer Bela Bezold draws on volcanic geology and biological cooperation as the conceptual foundation for a series of eyewear frames, arriving at shapes that feel both ancient and contemporary. The work is a good example of rigorous thematic research producing something that reads as intuitive rather than labored.

Creative Boom Career & Practice Surviving Redundancy: Tips on How to Cope, and Why It Can Be a Blessing in Disguise

Practical, honest writing about navigating job loss in the creative industries, from the immediate financial and emotional management to the longer-term reframe of what redundancy can open up. Less aspirational than most pieces on the subject; more useful for it.

Interface & UX — Lead
Netflix Tarot Design System by LOS YORK Is a Masterclass in Scale

LOS YORK designed a tarot-themed design system for Netflix that holds coherence across hundreds of touchpoints without losing visual tension. It is a rare example of a large-scale design system that was not flattened into safety during production, preserving the conceptual edge that made it worth building in the first place.

Abduzeedo

The challenge of scaling a design system is usually framed as a technical problem. This case study suggests it is primarily an editorial one: knowing what to protect and what to let go. The tarot metaphor was apparently not just decorative — it was load-bearing. Worth studying the decisions.
AI & Workflow Context Engineering: A Repeatable AI Workflow for Product Designers

A structured approach to working with AI tools in product design that centers the discipline of context construction rather than prompt improvisation. The argument is that the quality of AI output in design work is primarily a function of how well the human frames the situation, not how clever the query is.

UX Collective AI & Perception A Designer’s Field Report on the Iconic Blind Spot in AI World Models

A practical field report on the gap between how AI models process visual information and how humans actually recognize and use iconic imagery. The blind spot described is specific and useful for anyone building AI-assisted design tools or trying to understand why those tools keep failing in the same place.

UX Collective Interface & AI The Last Interface, Sycophancy in AI, Design Is How It Wins

An argument that the way AI systems are designed to respond — agreeable, confirming, frictionless — is not a UX flaw but a competitive strategy, and that the design choices encoding sycophancy are deliberate. The piece asks whether good design can correct for a business model that benefits from telling users what they want to hear.

UX Collective
Data & Behavior Your Phone Isn’t Eavesdropping. The Reality Is Stranger.

A rigorous look at why targeted ads feel psychic without microphone access, tracing the actual behavioral data pipelines that make the coincidences possible. The analysis is relevant for anyone designing systems that collect behavioral data, because users experience those systems through the conspiracy theory even when they know better.

UX Collective Branding 7MA Filmes: Bold Film Branding from Brazil

The visual identity for Brazilian production company 7MA Filmes deploys bold typographic decisions and high-contrast color work to signal a company that takes visual authorship seriously. Strong regional film branding tends to look like this: confident and specific, not trying to read as global.

Abduzeedo Web & 3D Three.js Conference Website Turns Community Into Living 3D UI

The conference website for a Three.js event uses the community itself as the visual material, rendering contributor data and participation as a living three-dimensional interface. A rare case where the medium is genuinely the message, and the technical execution is fully in service of that idea.
