Álvaro Urbano Suspends Fleeting Moments of Decay in Metal Plants

Madrid-based Urbano sculpts common houseplants from metal and paint, casting vulnerable, browning life forms into a permanent material — “small monuments of things that normally would disappear or change in a few days, or in minutes.” The result is sculpture that holds the exact instant between blossom and collapse.

Colossal

Drawing on theater and architecture, Urbano builds not just standalone objects but entire immersive scenes. His work asks what it means to preserve something that was never meant to last — and how that act changes the thing itself.

McKenzie Ellis (Mothica) on the Importance of Enjoying the Process

The Creative Independent interviews McKenzie Ellis, aka Mothica, who handles every aspect of her music and visual art herself — lyrics, production, video. She talks about the challenge of articulating creative choices and why she keeps a sense of humor central to work that is, on the surface, intensely personal.

The Creative Independent

The thread running through this interview: the process is the point. Ellis isn’t waiting to enjoy the work once it’s finished — she’s built a practice where the making is what sustains her. That’s the lesson.

Krakow is hosting its first International AI Art Biennale March 6–20, 2026 — bringing together 45 artists, researchers, and experts from six-plus countries for exhibitions, performances, and workshops focused on creative dialogue with AI. biennaleai.org

The Biennale format carries weight: it signals that AI art has moved past the “interesting experiment” phase and into the part of the art world where curators, collectors, and institutions start to care. Krakow is an interesting choice of venue — not a usual art capital, which might be the point.

Paper.design: GPU Shaders, MCP and Vibe Coding for Designers

Paper.design is a canvas-based design tool built on web standards — HTML and CSS natively, no export step required. It integrates GPU shaders and MCP agent workflows, built for a world where vibe coding and AI-assisted design are the new normal. Every artboard is already a step closer to shipping code.

Abduzeedo

The handoff problem has been killing design energy for decades. Paper.design’s bet is that making the canvas speak the language of the browser directly — not as export, but as native output — changes the whole relationship between designing and building.
UI Trends UI Trends You Will Likely See in 2026

Lummi maps the interface patterns gaining ground this year: storytelling-first UIs that unfold through scroll-based narrative and progressive disclosure, paired with a renewed emphasis on depth, texture, and kinetic typography that makes digital elements feel tactile. The flat era is receding.
