Art World Marica Vilcek, Graceful Champion of Immigrant Artists, Dies at 89

Marica Vilcek spent decades supporting immigrant artists in the United States through the Vilcek Foundation, which she co-founded with her husband Jan. Her legacy is measured not in the art she made but in the careers she helped build, quietly shaping the landscape of contemporary American art from behind the scenes.

Art World UK Museums Hold Over 260,000 Human Remains, Report Finds

A new report reveals that UK museums collectively hold more than 260,000 sets of human remains, many acquired during the colonial era under ethically questionable circumstances. The findings are renewing pressure on institutions to accelerate repatriation efforts and confront the uncomfortable origins of their collections.

Hyperallergic Sculpture Painterly Figures Entwine in Soojin Choi’s Ceramic Sculptures

Soojin Choi sculpts intertwined figures in clay that capture the tension between intimacy and entanglement, with surfaces painted in loose, expressive brushwork. The sculptures feel like frozen moments from paintings that decided to step off the canvas and occupy real space.

Colossal Photography Frank Relle’s Photos Revel in Louisiana’s Otherworldly Swampland

Frank Relle photographs Louisiana bayous at night, using artificial light to transform familiar swampland into scenes that look like they belong on another planet. The long exposures and careful lighting reveal textures and colors in the water and canopy that daylight would flatten into green-brown monotony.

Colossal Illustration Ambiguity Reigns in Olaf Hajek’s Mysterious Illustrations

Olaf Hajek builds surreal narrative worlds where human figures coexist with botanical forms and mythological fragments, all rendered in rich, saturated color. The illustrations resist easy interpretation, inviting viewers to construct their own stories from the visual clues scattered across each composition.
