Paint & Canvas
Paint & Canvas — Lead
Hurvin Anderson

Tate Britain is hosting the first major solo exhibition of British artist Hurvin Anderson, showcasing over 80 paintings from his career. His color-drenched landscapes and interiors explore themes of belonging and diaspora, layering locations and memories to examine personal and cultural heritage.

flolondon.co.uk

Anderson works slowly, deliberately. A show this size at Tate Britain is a landmark moment for a painter who has spent decades quietly building one of the most emotionally charged bodies of work in British art. If you’re in London this month, this is the one.
Exhibition Síomha Harrington: A Spy in the House of Love

Harrington’s UK solo debut stages intimacy through 12 small-scale paintings and a commanding central canvas, using latex gloves, ribbons, and choreographed gestures. The show draws the viewer into the mechanics of looking, exploring the interplay between agency and submission.

Dazed Exhibition Michaelina Wautier

A landmark exhibition at the Royal Academy presents the first UK show dedicated to the 17th-century Brussels artist Michaelina Wautier, overlooked for centuries. Ambitious history paintings, nuanced portraits, and striking allegories finally get their due.

nonsuchfoundation.com

The Creative Mind
The Creative Mind — Lead
Overcoming Creative Blocks: Practical Tips for Modern Artists

This piece explores the psychological roots of creative blocks — fear and perfectionism chief among them — and offers practical strategies for modern artists to sustain creativity. It emphasizes setting small, realistic goals, focusing on play over product, and accepting imperfection to reignite creative flow.

martha-ryan.com

The throughline: the block is almost never about lack of talent. It’s about the relationship between the maker and the outcome. Shift that relationship and the work starts moving again. Simple advice that actually holds up under pressure.
Community Run London / Draw London — Raising Money for Oxfam at the London Marathon Creative Boom Creative Practice The Artist’s Way: 13-Week Online Course — Unblock Creativity

This 13-week creative journey guides participants through Julia Cameron’s renowned framework to rediscover authentic meaning and access unlimited creativity. Supported by community and structured weekly rituals, it aims to dissolve blocks and connect each person to their unique creative voice.

Tech × Art
Tech × Art — Lead
Cathie Wood Calls Refik Anadol ‘One of the Most Amazing Artists’ as AI Art Sparks Fierce Debate

ARK Invest’s Cathie Wood praised Refik Anadol amidst an intensifying debate over whether AI expands or threatens human creativity. Anadol, known for merging data, architecture, and AI into immersive installations, defends the tool: art is defined by intention and emotion, not the brush.

unite.ai

The copyright and authorship questions aren’t going away — and when capital starts weighing in on which AI artists matter, it changes what gets made and who gets shown. Anadol is interesting precisely because he insists on the human frame inside the machine work.
Trend AI Image Trends in 2026

AI image generation is shifting toward authentic, human-centric aesthetics that embrace imperfection and emotional resonance, moving away from the over-polished. Creators are increasingly using AI for rapid ideation, then applying human refinement for emotional nuance — a genuine collaboration, not a shortcut.

LTX Studio via Benzinga Analysis AI Art Trends to Watch in 2026

As AI embeds deeper into professional art practice, the next phase marks a transition to human-AI synergy, with tools understanding artistic intent at near-human levels. Artists are focusing on personal storytelling to push back against homogenization in AI-produced work.

Interface & UX — Lead
Postlife Magazine Issue Two: Editorial Design

Abduzeedo spotlights the editorial design work behind Postlife Magazine’s second issue — a study in how print typography, grid systems, and image sequencing create a coherent visual world across a publication. Editorial design at its most intentional.

Abduzeedo

Magazine design is where layout thinking gets tested hardest — every spread is a composition decision, and rhythm across 80+ pages demands a system that bends without breaking. Postlife Issue Two is the kind of reference work that ends up dog-eared.
Tools WebGL Design Tool Unicorn Studio Brings Shaders to All

Unicorn Studio is putting GPU shader control into the hands of designers who don’t write GLSL — a visual canvas for building the kind of motion and material effects that used to require a specialist. Real-time WebGL, no code required.

Abduzeedo Essay The Last Interface

UX Collective tackles what happens when AI agents replace the traditional UI layer entirely. If the interface becomes a conversation, what do visual designers actually design? A sharp provocation about where the discipline is heading.
