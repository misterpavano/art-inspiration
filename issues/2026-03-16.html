Oscar-nominee Pen Densham on Why Holding Back Is the Worst Mistake a Creative Can Make

Pen Densham, Oscar-nominated filmmaker and photographer, makes the case that self-censorship kills creative work faster than failure ever could. He draws from decades of professional output to argue that the instinct to hold back is a learned behavior, and that unlearning it is the actual craft.

Creative Boom

The interesting thing here isn’t the advice itself — it’s who’s giving it. Densham has built a career across film, photography, and storytelling, and his argument isn’t motivational-poster optimism. It’s grounded in the specific cost of restraint: the work that never gets made, the voice that never finds its register.

A campaign challenging casual use of a slur that still does real damage — and the design and messaging work behind it. The piece examines how creative teams can build something that lands without feeling preachy, and what it takes to shift language at scale.

Corentin Bernadou’s WebGL Portfolio Blends Swiss Design and Code

Corentin Bernadou’s portfolio uses WebGL to merge tight Swiss typographic systems with real-time generative rendering — the result is a site that feels both rigorously structured and genuinely alive. It’s one of those rare pieces of web work that treats the browser as a proper design medium.

Abduzeedo

The Swiss design tradition is all grids, hierarchy, and constraint — not typically what you reach for when you want something to feel dynamic. The interesting move here is using those constraints as the scaffold for the WebGL work, so the motion feels earned rather than decorative. Worth studying if you build anything for the web.
UX & Behavior Your Phone Isn’t Eavesdropping. The Reality Is Stranger.

The phone-eavesdropping belief is nearly universal, and it’s wrong — but what’s actually happening is more unsettling. This piece unpacks the behavioral inference systems that make targeted ads feel psychic without any audio required, and what that means for how we design honest products.

UX Collective Digital Art Rik Oostenbroek Digital Art Wallpapers Store

Rik Oostenbroek launches a wallpaper store carrying his signature digital art — fluid organic forms, rich color gradients, and an aesthetic that sits somewhere between biology and abstraction. High-quality work from one of the more distinctive voices in commercial digital art.

Abduzeedo Typography Fontshare Free Fonts: 100 Quality Typefaces for Designers

Fontshare now offers 100 typefaces at no cost — and unlike most free font repositories, the quality bar is high enough to use in real client work. A practical resource worth bookmarking if you do any visual design work at all.
