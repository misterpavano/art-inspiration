Total creative freedom: Andy Vella’s posters for the Teenage Cancer Trust 2026 concerts

Award-winning designer Andy Vella received a rare brief for the Teenage Cancer Trust’s 2026 concert series: no brief at all. The resulting poster campaign is a case study in what happens when a designer is trusted enough to go wherever the work needs to go.
