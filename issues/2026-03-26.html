Featured — Sculpture
Explore a Growing City of Meticulously Crafted Miniature Paper Buildings by Charles Young

Charles Young constructs an ever-expanding metropolis entirely from paper, with each miniature building rendered in obsessive architectural detail that rewards close looking. The cumulative effect transforms a modest craft material into something genuinely monumental.

Colossal

What makes this project compelling is its open-ended ambition. Young treats paper architecture not as a finished statement but as an ongoing urban simulation, adding structures the way a real city grows, one block at a time.
Fine Art Tracey Emin’s Cult of the Self

Hyperallergic examines how Tracey Emin has built an entire artistic practice around radical autobiography, turning personal confession into a form of cultural authority. The question is whether the cult of the self can sustain itself when the confessions start to repeat.

Hyperallergic Painting Anki King’s Nordic Noir

Anki King’s paintings channel the brooding atmospheric tension of Nordic noir into visual form, creating images that feel like stills from a thriller that never quite reveals its plot. The darkness here is tonal and psychological, not just a matter of low light.

Hyperallergic Criticism Social Malpractice in the Age of Cultural Compliance

A sharp critique of how institutional pressure toward cultural compliance is producing a new kind of social malpractice within the art world. The essay argues that consensus-seeking has become its own form of creative suppression.

Hyperallergic
Art World Lebanese Artist Ali Sbeity Reportedly Killed in Israeli Strike

Hyperallergic reports on the death of Lebanese artist Ali Sbeity, whose work explored themes of identity and displacement in the Middle East. The loss underscores the ongoing human cost of conflict on creative communities in the region.

Hyperallergic Photography Camille Lemoine’s Portrait of Home and Belonging in Rural Scotland

Camille Lemoine photographs the quiet textures of rural Scottish life with an outsider’s eye and an insider’s patience, building a portrait of belonging that never sentimentalizes its subject. The work treats landscape and domestic space as extensions of the same emotional territory.

Colossal Installation A Lush Textile Installation Springs to Life in Shanghai

Hu Yuehua weaves an immersive textile environment in Shanghai that blurs the line between natural growth and handcrafted fabrication. The installation envelops visitors in a dense canopy of fiber and form that feels simultaneously botanical and architectural.

Featured — Brand Stunt
Cushelle Made the World’s First Magazine Out of Toilet Paper, and Yes, You Can Flush It

Cushelle has produced a fully readable magazine printed entirely on toilet paper, designed to be flushed after reading. It is the kind of brand stunt that commits so fully to its own absurdity that it loops back around to being genuinely clever.

Creative Boom

The project works because it takes the disposability of its medium as a feature rather than a limitation. Every editorial decision, from layout to content length, is shaped by the knowledge that the reader will literally flush the finished product.
Industry Roundup Booms & Shakes: March’s Fresh Moves, New Launches and a 26-Year Goodbye

Creative Boom rounds up the most significant moves across the creative industry this month, including new studio launches, notable closures, and the end of a 26-year run for one established name. A snapshot of an industry that keeps reinventing itself while quietly mourning what it loses.

Creative Boom Branding Sunday’s ‘Playful Precision’: A Robotics Brand Designed to Give You Your Time Back

Sunday’s brand identity positions consumer robotics as friendly and approachable rather than coldly futuristic, using playful visual language to make precision technology feel human. The branding challenge here is making people trust a robot by making it look like it does not take itself too seriously.

Creative Boom Brand Refresh How Taxi Studio Gave Warburtons a Birthday Makeover Worthy of 150 Years

Taxi Studio redesigns Warburtons’ packaging for the bakery’s 150th anniversary, balancing heritage cues with contemporary shelf presence. The trick with legacy brand refreshes is respecting what customers already trust while quietly modernizing everything around it.

Featured — Design Tools
Omma AI Canvas by Spline Generates Interactive 3D Web Experiences

Spline’s Omma AI Canvas lets designers generate interactive 3D web experiences through an AI-driven workflow that collapses what used to be weeks of WebGL development into rapid iteration. The tool treats 3D web design as a creative medium rather than an engineering problem.

Abduzeedo

The real shift here is accessibility. Interactive 3D on the web has been locked behind a technical barrier that kept most designers out. If this tool delivers on its promise, the aesthetic vocabulary of the web is about to get significantly more dimensional.
AI & Design What AI Exposes About Design

UX Collective argues that AI is not replacing design so much as revealing which parts of the design process were always mechanical and which require genuine creative judgment. The exposure is uncomfortable for anyone who built a career on the mechanical parts.

UX Collective Design Process The Three Thirds

A framework for dividing creative work into three equal phases, each demanding a different mode of thinking and a different relationship to the material. The model is simple enough to actually use, which puts it ahead of most design process frameworks.

UX Collective Creative Coding How to Build a Three.js Fluid Reveal Effect with WebGPU

A technical walkthrough of building a fluid reveal animation using Three.js and WebGPU, pushing browser-based graphics into territory that recently required native applications. The tutorial treats the technical implementation as inseparable from the aesthetic outcome.

Abduzeedo App Design Nite Event Discovery App Design Puts Nightlife First

Nite’s interface design prioritizes the specific needs of nightlife discovery, treating event browsing as a fundamentally different interaction pattern than daytime activity search. Dark UI, time-sensitive information hierarchy, and social context drive every design decision.
